## A: wrong teaching that would mislead

**A1. fr-nomi, lesson "Genere" paragraph 2: "-age, -ment, -eur, -isme sono maschili".**
- It contradicts the same module's first paragraph (la fleur, la couleur, la chaleur) and quiz q1 (which says fleur/couleur/chaleur/douleur are feminine).
- Abstract nouns in -eur are feminine: la peur, la douleur, la valeur, la hauteur, la largeur, la longueur, la profondeur, la lueur, la rigueur. Only agent nouns in -eur are masculine (le moteur, le serveur, le directeur).
- Fix: "-eur: maschili se indicano chi/che cosa fa (le moteur, le serveur); femminili se astratti/qualità (la couleur, la chaleur, la peur, la douleur)".

**A2. fr-falsiamici, Verbi table: "causer | chiacchierare; causare = provoquer".**
- It implies causer does not mean "causare". In fact causer = to cause (causer un accident, causer des problèmes) is its main sense; "chatter" is only the colloquial second sense.
- Fix: "causer: causare (causer un accident) e, familiare, chiacchierare (causer avec qqn)". It is a partial false friend, not a total one.

## B: imprecise, incomplete, or arguable key

**B1. fr-etre, section "Avoir: espressioni", intro sentence.**
- "ecco quelle in cui l'italiano userebbe «essere»" is false for Italian: every row (faim, soif, chaud, froid, peur, sommeil, raison...) uses avere in Italian too. The trap exists for English speakers ("I am hot"), not for Italians.
- The heading "(non «essere»!)" and the trap "ho caldo = j'ai chaud (non je suis chaud)" have the same problem.
- Fix: say that Italian and French coincide here; the real traps are "avoir envie de / besoin de / mal à" and "il fait chaud" vs "j'ai chaud".

**B2. fr-presente, card c1: "parle, parles, parle, parlons, parlez, parlent: tre forme su sei suonano [PARL]".**
- It is 4 of 6 (parle, parles, parle, parlent). The lesson text is right (je/tu/il/ils).

**B3. fr-imparfait, card c2: "Tre forme suonano [È]".**
- It is 4: -ais, -ais, -ait, -aient. The same card lists all four endings.

**B4. fr-imparfait, practice p3.**
- It "corrects" "j'ai habité à Lyon quand j'étais enfant" to "j'habitais...". The original is a fully acceptable sentence (a closed period of residence, passé composé).
- A learner is taught a false rule here. Either change the exercise's sentence (use something unambiguous, e.g. "j'ai habité à Lyon depuis 2010") or give the note: "entrambi possibili; l'imparfait descrive l'abitudine di allora, il passé composé un periodo concluso".

**B5. fr-imparfait, quiz q2, distractor B "Il a plu quand je sortais."**
- It is grammatical (it rained while I was going out) and not just "inverted": the meaning differs. The question says "Quale frase è giusta?" and the key is only the best choice.
- Fix: reword the question to "Quale frase significa 'pioveva quando sono uscito'?".

**B6. fr-subj2, quiz q5 and its explanation: "L'indicatif ai è usato nel parlato ma non è la norma".**
- After a superlative or "le seul / le premier", both mood forms are correct. The subjonctif is used when the speaker is expressing a judgement; the indicatif is normal when stating a fact ("c'est le plus beau voyage que j'ai fait").
- With "aie" as the only key, the item punishes a correct answer.
- Fix: say "il subjonctif è la scelta tipica quando c'è un giudizio personale; l'indicatif è possibile". Same in the lesson's relative-clause list ("Dopo superlativi... subjonctif").

**B7. Respelling of "yeux" is wrong in three places (and inconsistent with fr-nomi).**
- fr-voc-vita table: "l'œil / les yeux | LÖY / lé-ZYË"; the explanation of q9 repeats "[lé-ZYË]".
- fr-registres idiom table: "coûter les yeux de la tête | [ku-té lé-zyë dë la TÈT]".
- Per the legend, ë = e muta. "Yeux" is [zjø], i.e. "ZYÖ" (as correctly given in fr-nomi: [dé-ZYÖ]).
- Fix all three to ZYÖ.

**B8. fr-voc-lavoro, "l'emploi (m.)" pron "lãn-PLUA".**
- There is no linking n after a nasal: l'emploi = [lã-PLUA]. (The n appears only after "un": un emploi [ẽ-nã-PLUA].)

**B9. fr-falsiamici, Verbi: "prévenir | avvisare (prevenire = empêcher)".**
- Prévenir also means "prevenire" (prévenir une maladie, la prévention; "mieux vaut prévenir que guérir"). So it is a partial false friend: both senses exist.

**B10. fr-esame, "Strategie generali" CO: "ogni audio è ascoltato due volte".**
- True for A2 and for most documents. At B1/B2 some documents are heard only once (e.g. the shorter documents in B2 CO ex. 2). Nuance it ("la maggior parte due volte, alcuni una sola: leggi le istruzioni").
- I am confident about B2; for B1 I am less sure (see unsure list).

**B11. fr-dialoghi, quiz q7: "Je voudrais fixer un rendez-vous" is treated as wrong.**
- "Fixer un rendez-vous" is perfectly good French (fixer une date / un rendez-vous). It is slightly less usual in the patient's mouth than "prendre rendez-vous", but the question is "Come si dice «Vorrei fissare un appuntamento»?", so two options are correct.
- Fix: replace the distractor (e.g. "Je voudrais faire un rendez-vous", a real error) or change the question to "la formula più naturale".

**B12. fr-connecteurs, quiz q5 and the indirect-speech rule.**
- The lesson states "demain → le lendemain" as absolute, and q5 treats "Il a dit qu'il viendrait demain" as wrong. If the report is made the same day (or demain is still in the future for the speaker) "demain" stays and is correct.
- Fix: add "se il giorno di riferimento è ancora futuro, demain resta".

## C: minor

- **C1. fr-nomi, plural table "-eau, -eu → +x".** Overgeneralises: bleu → bleus, pneu → pneus, landau(s), lieu (the fish) → lieus. Add as exceptions.
- **C2. fr-saluti, card c11 "la x/t finale si lega con z davanti a vocale".** The liaison of vingt-et-un is t, not z, and "huit" is not a liaison at all in the example. Only dix-huit / dix-neuf have z (from the x).
- **C3. fr-etre, "Liaison obbligatoria con avoir e être: ... elle est [è-LÈ]".** That is enchaînement (the l is already pronounced), not liaison.
- **C4. fr-futur, lesson "Differenza dall'italiano: dopo si mai futuro né condizionale".** Add "nella condizione" or point to the exception of indirect questions (it is only in the fr-cond module: "je me demande si tu viendras").
- **C5. fr-futur, quiz q3 distractor "Si j'avais eu le temps, je voyagerais".** It is a valid mixed conditional (if I had had time [back then], I would travel [now]). Rare, but not "wrong".
- **C6. fr-quotidiano, preposition table "en: ... stagione".** "En été / en automne / en hiver", but "au printemps". Add the exception.
- **C7. fr-quotidiano, q5.** "Je vais au médecin" is called "incorretto". It is colloquial/regional (very common in Québec and Belgium); "familiare, da evitare all'esame" would be more accurate.
- **C8. fr-pronomi, "penser à, rêver à, faire attention à, s'intéresser à + persona".** The standard verb for dreaming about a person is rêver de (rêver de qqn); "rêver à" means daydream about/consider. Replace rêver à with another verb (songer à, tenir à, croire en...) or specify "rêver de lui".
- **C9. fr-drills, practice p4: "Il est quelle heure ?" as familiar.** The usual familiar forms are "Quelle heure il est ?" / "Il est quelle heure ?" (the latter is acceptable but marked) / "Tu as l'heure ?".
- **C10. fr-drills, practice p7: "Nota: pas le temps (determinato dopo il non-partitivo)".** The note is muddled: "avoir le temps" is a fixed expression with the definite article; the negative keeps it.
- **C11. fr-voc-viaggi, "le quai | il binario / la banchina" and trap note "Le quai = binario".** At the station quai = platform (marciapiede/banchina) and the track number is voie (binario). The dialogue in fr-dialoghi correctly says "voie quatre" = "binario quattro", so the course contradicts itself. Say "quai = banchina; voie = binario".
- **C12. Notation inconsistencies a learner may stumble on.**
  - quatre-vingts is [ka-trë-VẼ] in fr-saluti but [ka-tr-VẼ] in fr-variantes (table and p1) and fr-esame.
  - "j'ai" is [zhé] in the grammar modules but [zhè] in many dialogue and vocabulary lines (zhè bë-ZUẼ, zhè ré-zèr-VÉ, zhè mal...). Also "personne [pèr-SON]" should be SÒN, and "-ions" is -LYÕ in A modules but -LIÕ in subj1 / cond.
- **C13. fr-imparfait / fr-futur / q4 + q5.** The distinction -ais [È] vs -ai [É] is presented as a fact ("l'unica differenza udibile"). Many Paris speakers merge the two (as the header note in francese.a.ts admits; only fr-cond mentions it). Add one line in the first place it appears.
- **C14. fr-variantes, Africa paragraph.** Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia are listed among countries where French is "lingua ufficiale o di istruzione". It is not official there; "lingua di grande diffusione/istruzione" is closer. The "oltre 20 paesi" claim refers to official French in sub-Saharan states.
- **C15. fr-voc-vita, q4 distractor "J'ai mal de tête".** Wrong in the intended sense, but "J'ai un mal de tête" / "avoir mal de tête" alone is not totally impossible. Acceptable; no change needed unless you want a cleaner distractor.

## Unsure (not reported as errors)

1. fr-variantes / fr-falsiamici, meals in Switzerland: the lesson groups Switzerland with Belgium and Québec for "déjeuner = colazione". In French Switzerland "petit-déjeuner" is the usual word for breakfast and "déjeuner" is often lunch for many speakers; I think the claim is true for Belgium and Québec only. Worth checking, and phrasing "in Belgio e Québec" / "in parte della Svizzera".
2. fr-voc-lavoro, trap note: "la note (e anche «conto» in Belgio)". "La note" = bill is Swiss (and Belgian use is not one I can confirm).
3. fr-esame, B1 CO listening count, see B10.
4. fr-voc-viaggi, "le feu rouge | il semaforo": strictly feu rouge = luce rossa, while "le feu / les feux (tricolores)" = semaforo. Fine for A2 use.
5. fr-subj1: "il semble que" + subjonctif is the standard, but the indicative is also found nowadays; the lesson's rule is the safe, prescriptive one.
6. fr-confronti, q8 explanation: the "en = mezzi chiusi, à = mezzi su cui si sale" rule is a convenient mnemonic with exceptions (en bateau, en métro, à moto / en moto, en avion). Fine as a mnemonic.
7. fr-dialoghi, restaurant: "Voici la carte" is translated "Ecco il menu", while "le menu à vingt euros" is the set menu; the course does not explain the carte / menu distinction. Not wrong, but a trap Italians fall into.
