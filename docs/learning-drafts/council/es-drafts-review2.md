# Review 2 of the Spanish C modules (independent)

Scope: es-indefiniti, es-numeri2, es-cambi, es-relativi, es-funciones, es-natura (spagnolo.c.1-4.ts).
Scores: accuracy 94/100, phonetics 9/10 (before fixes; all A and B fixed in place, ids unchanged).

## A (factual / rule errors)
- es-indefiniti, "Esclamativi" paragraph: said Italian puts the adjective AFTER the noun in "che bella casa". Italian puts it before; Spanish puts it after the noun (with más/tan). Rewritten.

## B (wrong or misleading, must fix)
- es-indefiniti: "No solo canta, sino también baila" -> with a conjugated verb the standard is "sino que también baila". Fixed.
- es-cambi, trap 4: garbled text ("senza sfumatura di colpa non mia"). Rewritten (se me olvidaron = sfuggite, olvidé / me olvidé de = colpa mia).
- es-funciones p5: Italian prompt "Non sono d'accordo fino a un certo punto" is incoherent; now "Sono d'accordo fino a un certo punto".
- es-natura respelling: "desde" [DES-de] -> [DES-dhe] (d after s = dh per legend). Same for césped [THES-ped] -> [THES-pedh] (final d = dh, cf. ciudad [thiu-DADH]).
- es-natura trap on gender: "El mar, ma la sal, la sangre" was confusing; rewritten (la mar is nautical/poetic; la sal, la sangre are feminine, unlike Italian).
- es-natura dialogue: "en los Picos de Europa hay osos y lobos" is too strong (bears only in the western/Asturian-Leonese part, rare); replaced with "en la cordillera Cantábrica hay osos y lobos" (true: Cantabrian brown bear and Iberian wolf).
- es-natura q1: distractor "el mosco" is a real word (mosquito in parts of Latin America); replaced with "la avispa".
- es-natura q2 explanation: said "pesca" can be the fruit; the fruit is "melocotón/durazno". Fixed.
- es-natura p2: "la mosca non punge" is false (horseflies, stable flies bite); softened.
- es-natura: búho = gufo (civetta is mochuelo); removed "civetta".

## C (minor, applied when cheap)

- es-indefiniti p4: "No imaginas lo cansado que estoy" -> Italian prompt person aligned ("quanto sono stanco").
- es-natura q10: redundant explanation sentence trimmed.
- es-funciones: respellings do not mark dh for word-initial d after a vowel across words (te diré, del dicho); consistent with the isolated-word legend, left as is. "ser pan comido" / "no hay mal": content monosyllables not capitalised consistently; left (single stressed syllable per phrase is readable).
