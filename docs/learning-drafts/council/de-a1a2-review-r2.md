## Legend (pron.ts PRON_FULL / PRON_SHORT)
Internally consistent: ng never split, É:/È:/E, ë, j, CAPS, ʼ, -er = a, ß = ss, z = voiced s all stated and exemplified correctly (Straße [SHTRA:-ssë], Sonne [ZO-në], Lehrer [LÉ:-ra]).
- C-L1: the legend says "ß si scrive ss" and "ss = s sorda", but final voiceless s is written three ways: Fuß [FU:SS], Fluss [FLUSS], heiß [HAISS] (double) versus muss [MUS], das [DAS], Haus [HAUS] (single). Say once: "in fine di parola ss e s si scrivono uguali" or normalise (Fluss [FLUS]). Cosmetic.
- C-L2: legend says "una sola sillaba maiuscola per parola" and then allows secondary stress in compounds, yet Auf Wiedersehen [AUF VI:-da-ZÉ:-ën] has two capitals inside one word. Allowed by the compound exception; keep but mention.
- C-L3: "ʼ" is only used in de-pron; Aus/Arzt/Amerika elsewhere never carry it (fine for an optional sign; legend should say "usato solo come esercizio nel modulo di pronuncia").
