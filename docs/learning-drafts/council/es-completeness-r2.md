## What still lacks to reach (and hold) 90% robustly
Above the line only on a thin margin (73 vs 72 needed; 72.5 if item 7 is judged half). Remaining sub-1 items:
1. **14 vocabulary theme lines at 0.5** (casa, famiglia, vestiti, meteo, shopping, trasporti, città, emergenze, banca/posta, studio, tecnologia, opinioni/sentimenti, politica/economia, connettori di conversazione): each is one 12-21 word table. Fix: 40+ words each, a mini-dialogue per theme and 2-3 theme-specific quiz/practice items. Even 6 themes would add +3. This is the main gap for B1-B2 depth.
2. **Lenizione b/d/g, -s finale/aspirata** (0.5): add a short h3 in es-fonetica2 (g between vowels, final -s/-d weak, s before consonant in Andalusia/Caribbean) with 2 quiz items and a drill: +0.5.
3. **Ortografia writing rules** (0.5): b/v and g/j choice rules, h words, abbreviations (Sr., Sra., Ud./Uds., Dr., pág., EE. UU.) with a dictation-style practice: +0.5.
4. Soft spots not scored below 1 but thin: idioms/proverbs exercises (2 cards, 1 practice, no quiz; real proverbs few), negation/prepositions short paragraphs, es-dialoghi has no L2->IT comprehension questions. Reinforcing these protects items 7, 29 and B idioms against a stricter grader.
