## A: wrong teaching that would mislead

**A-1. es-registri, lesson, "Email e lettere formali" (Saluto bullet).**
Text: "A quien corresponda: (dopo i due punti, la riga successiva con la minuscola)".
Wrong. RAE Ortografía 2010: after the colon that closes a letter or email salutation (*Estimado señor López:*), the text starts with a **capital letter**, usually on a new line. The module's own example does this ("Estimado señor López: Le escribo…", also p1 "Estimados señores: Me dirijo…"), so the lesson contradicts itself. Fix: "dopo i due punti, la riga successiva inizia con la maiuscola". Card es-registri c1 ("si va a capo") is fine.

**A-2. es-saluti, lesson, "Giorni, mesi, data".**
Text: "Si usano i numeri cardinali (el uno de mayo, non «el primero»: …)".
Wrong as a prohibition. For the 1st of the month both are correct and **el primero de mayo is very common** (Spain and LATAM; *el Primero de Mayo* is the holiday's name). Cardinals are the rule for 2nd to 31st. Fix: "il primo del mese si può dire el uno o el primero de mayo; dal 2 in poi solo cardinali". Card c7 and the quiz (es-saluti q6, 10 November) are not affected.

## B: imprecise or incomplete

**B-1. Pronunciation notation is inconsistent between module groups (affects what the learner actually learns).**
The course intro and es-pron define: `y` = consonant i (calle [KA-ye], llave [YA-be]), `ss` = voiceless s (casa [KA-ssa], queso [KE-sso]), `dh` = soft d. The later modules use a different legend:
- es-voc-vita, es-voc-viaggi, es-voc-lavoro, es-dialoghi, es-esame, es-latam use **`j` for ll/y** ("j = ll/y, come la gi di gita"): llave [JA-be], calle [KA-je], pollo [PO-jo], cuchillo [ku-CI-jo], billete [bi-JE-te], caballo [ka-BA-jo]. Two different rules for one sound, and `j` is not in the course-intro legend. "gi di gita" is an affricate, which is coarser than the real sound.
- Those same modules write **single `s` between vowels**: queso [KE-so], casa [KA-sa], mesa [ME-sa] (es-esame, "Vocali pure"), desayuno [de-sa-JU-no], camisa [ka-MI-sa], pasaporte [pa-sa-POR-te], museo. This re-creates the northern-Italian voiced-s error ([KA-za]) that es-pron warns against, and es-esame even teaches casa [KA-sa] right after the course spent a module on [KA-ssa].
- `dh` is dropped there (verdad [ber-DAD], estoy de acuerdo [a-KUER-do], TAR-des in es-dialoghi, ciudad [thiu-DAD]) while es-pron/es-ser use [ber-DADH], [a-KUER-dho].
Fix: one legend for all modules (y, ss, dh), or a regenerate pass. The b.* header comment itself says "Align with the es-pron legend if it differs": it does.

**B-2. es-passiva, lesson "Trappole" + q5 explanation + header: "estoy por salir".**
The module says `*estoy por` is wrong for "sto per uscire" and that "Estoy por salir esiste in alcune zone ma non ha il senso comune di sto per". In Latin America (Argentina, Chile, Mexico, etc.) *estar por + infinitivo* does mean "estar a punto de" (DPD: "estoy por irme"). In Spain it means "tener ganas / estar aún por hacer". Not an error to mark with `*`. Fix: "a punto de è la forma sicura e universale; estar por + inf. in America può valere 'sto per', in Spagna ha altri sensi". The key of q5 stays correct.

**B-3. es-falsiamici q3 ("Cosa significa vaso?").**
Key "Bicchiere" is right, but the distractor "Vaso sanguigno" is also a genuine meaning (*vaso sanguíneo*, anatomical sense in DLE). Replace the distractor (e.g. with "Pentola" or "Barattolo") or reword the stem as "Come si dice 'bicchiere'?".

**B-4. es-registri q3 ("diminutivo di madre"), distractor "madrita".**
*madrecita* is the textbook form, but *madrita* is attested colloquially (Mexico, Caribbean, Spain). The explanation says the other forms "suonano scorrette". Safer: swap the distractor (e.g. "madreta") or soften the explanation.

**B-5. es-falsiamici, table row "encontrar: trovare (incontrare = quedar con)".**
*incontrare* (meet someone) is *encontrarse con / ver / conocer*; *quedar con* means "arrange to meet". Row should be "encontrar = trovare; incontrare = encontrarse con / ver".

**B-6. es-falsiamici, counts.**
Lead and subtitle promise "Oltre 80 parole traditrici" and "Altre decine di falsi amici sono nelle flashcard". The table has 49 rows and the flashcards (c1 to c11) only repeat those same words. Fix the claim ("circa 50") or add the missing words.

**B-7. Inflated word counts in vocabulary modules.**
es-voc-vita says "Circa 100 parole" (table rows: 74). es-voc-viaggi says "Più di 90 parole" (70). es-voc-lavoro says "Circa 100 termini" (60). Adjust the text to the real numbers or add vocabulary (see completeness gaps below).

**B-8. es-subj1 + c6, "Ojalá … Non vuole que" / "Senza que".**
RAE accepts and the spoken language uses *ojalá que llueva* as well as *ojalá llueva*. "Non vuole que" is too strong; same in es-cond p4 ("senza que"). Fix: "ojalá regge il subjuntivo; que è facoltativo".

**B-9. es-trapas, "Concordanza dei tempi" table.**
Simplified to the point of being wrong at the edges: after **pretérito perfecto** the imperfect subjunctive is also normal when the action is past ("Me ha pedido que viniera"; "Me ha dicho que se fuera"). The table sends perfecto only to the present-subjunctive column. Add a note.

**B-10. es-nomi lesson + c2: "parole greche in -ma … el mapa, el día".**
*mapa* and *día* are not Greek -ma words (Latin *mappa*, *dies*); they are just masculine exceptions in -a. List them separately: "maschili in -a: el día, el mapa, el planeta, el mapa…".

**B-11. es-connettori q8, distractor "Me preguntó que dónde vivía".**
Treated as an error in the explanation ("errore comune perché si ricalca l'italiano"). It is a colloquial Spanish structure (*que* of repetition/dequeísmo-adjacent), not an Italianism. The key is right; only the explanation's reason is off.

**B-12. es-falsiamici q5 ("Sei mai stato…?"), explanation "Nunca è solo per le frasi negative: ¿has estado nunca? è un calco".**
*¿Has estado nunca en…?* is uncommon but not ungrammatical (interrogative *nunca/jamás* = "alguna vez" is recorded in the DLE for *jamás*; for *nunca* it is attested in Spain). Key (alguna vez) is correct and the best answer; soften the explanation to "poco naturale, preferisci alguna vez" (also marked "unsure" below).

## C: minor

- **C-1** es-saluti lesson: the aside "usted … è un pronome con ruolo ampio" is vague; "es la una / son las dos" is fine. Decimal/thousands note ("Spagna: punto per le migliaia") ignores that RAE recommends a thin space and accepts both point and comma as decimal sign.
- **C-2** es-pron: "la s è sempre sorda". Before a voiced consonant (*mismo, desde, asno*) it is [z]. The simplification is pedagogically fine for Italians but literally "sempre" is inexact.
- **C-3** es-voc-viaggi: "el andén = binario". *andén* is the platform (banchina); the track is *la vía*. Fine as a loose gloss, but "binario" in Italian train announcements is the platform number.
- **C-4** es-drills example: "con este clima" for "con questo tempo". Weather = *tiempo*; *clima* is climate (accepted in some LATAM speech, but not what the lesson teaches).
- **C-5** es-porpara: "Per l'età e la dimensione «normali» si usa más grande / más pequeño" is garbled. *mayor/menor* = older/younger (and larger/smaller in formal register); *más grande/pequeño* = bigger/smaller (and older in LATAM). Reword.
- **C-6** es-latam q8 explanation: "il voseo … tipici del Río de la Plata". Voseo is also central American, so "solo Río de la Plata" is too narrow (the lesson body is correct).
- **C-7** es-voc-vita p5: "En el desayuno bebo leche…" is acceptable, but "Para el desayuno / Desayuno leche…" is more natural for "a colazione".
- **C-8** es-imperfecto lesson: "Sono regolari tutti i verbi, anche quelli con dittongazione" is followed by "I tre irregolari". Fine, but the first sentence should say "tutti tranne ser, ir, ver".
- **C-9** es-subj2 lesson lists *mientras* with *cuando* for future: right, but *mientras que* ("whereas") takes the indicative; worth one line.
- **C-10** es-ser table "cansado: ser = stancante": *ser cansado* is rare (*es un trabajo cansado* works; for persons use *cansino* or *pesado*). Low priority.
