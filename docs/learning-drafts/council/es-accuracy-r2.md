### B (imprecise / defensible distractor)
- **B-1. es-futuro, q6** ("sono appena arrivato"), distractor `Llego recién`.
  `llego recién` / `recién llego` is natural Latin American Spanish for "I have just arrived", so it is a second correct answer for a LATAM reader. Fix: replaced with `Acabo llegar` (missing *de*, a real Italian-speaker error). FIXED.
- **B-2. es-esame, lesson "Pronuncia e prova orale", bullet "Niente doppie italiane".**
  Gives `pizza` [PI-tha]. Spanish speakers say pizza with [ts] (or [s] in America), not th; and the example is not about a Spanish word with a single consonant. Fix: replaced with `cama` [KA-ma] (non «camma»). FIXED.

### C (minor, mostly left)
- **C-1 es-voc-vita, "el recibo = scontrino"**: *recibo* is the receipt in general (in Spain a till slip is often *ticket*). Fixed to "ricevuta, scontrino". FIXED.
- **C-2 es-voc-viaggi, "la urgencia = pronto soccorso"**: the department is normally plural, *las urgencias*. FIXED (word and respelling [ur-HEN-thias]).
- **C-3 es-ser, table + c6 "cansado: ser = stancante"**: *ser cansado* is rare in Spain, usable in LATAM (*es un trabajo cansado*); low priority (same as r1 C-10). Not changed.
- **C-4 es-quotidiano, Domande**: "Il soggetto va dopo il verbo" is a strong tendency (not a rule) with interrogatives; fine for A1. Not changed.
- **C-5 es-pron, dittonghi**: "Due vocali deboli diverse formano un dittongo con accento sulla seconda" is true only when the word is stressed there (ciudad, cuidado is stressed on *da*). Wording is loose, not wrong. Not changed.
