## de-nomi
Unsure claim 9 (header): gender tendencies (-e f, -er m, -um n): fine as tendencies; but note below.
Findings:
- [B] Gender table: "das (n): ... -um (das Datum)" without the counter-rule that -tum is masculine (der Reichtum, der Irrtum, der Besitztum) and -us is masculine (der Zirkus, der Mechanismus). Learners will wrongly say "das Eigentum" (this one is indeed neuter, which makes the rule worse). Add "-tum = quasi sempre der (eccezioni das Eigentum, das Christentum)". Also "-er masculine" has many neuter exceptions (das Wasser, das Fenster, das Zimmer, das Messer) that are not listed.
- [B] c3 "Suffissi neutri: ... Ge-": Ge- is a prefix and unreliable (der Gesang, der Geschmack, die Gebühr, die Geschichte, der Gedanke); the lesson says "tendenze" but the card says "Suffissi neutri" as if a rule. Soften on the card.
- [C] Plural table "Stadt -> die Städte [SHTÈ-të]": the vowel mark "È" without ":" is not in the legend (È: long, E short). Use SHTE-të (short, as the header itself says).
- [C] "alcuni maschili deboli in -en: den Studenten, den Jungen, den Herrn": the ending is -n/-en (Herr -> Herrn); wording "in -en" refers to the accusative ending, ambiguous.
- [C] Plural of "die Stadt" is classed in the "-e (± umlaut)" type in the table and fine; p2 ok.
Everything else checked and correct (articles, plural patterns, kein-, possessives, adjective endings in the examples, composites, pronunciations).

## de-presente
Unsure claim 10 (header): du tanzt, reist, heißt (no extra s), isst (essen), weißt (wissen): all correct.
Findings:
- [B] "Niente forma progressiva" / q6 expl "Il tedesco non ha la forma progressiva" and c11: overstated. - [C] q6 distractor "Ich bin gerade essen" and "Ich esse am essen" are not standard (the second needs "am Essen"); fine as wrong answers. Key correct.

## de-futuro
Unsure claim 15 (header): "werden + wohl" for probability OK; würde + infinitive avoided with haben/sein/modals is the standard recommendation (spoken language does use "ich würde gern haben/ich würde können" occasionally). Correct as stated.
Findings:
- [B] "Con gli altri verbi: würde + infinito (Ich würde kommen, non «ich käme»)": the "non" makes "ich käme" look wrong. It is perfectly correct (and normal in writing / for common strong verbs: käme, ginge, bliebe, fände, wüsste, ließe, gäbe, täte), just more formal; würde+infinitive is the preferred spoken form for weak verbs (where the KII is identical to the Präteritum: *ich machte* for *ich würde machen*). ## de-nebensaetze2
Unsure claim (header): "weil ... hat kommen können" (haben before the double infinitive in subordinate clauses) is correct and normal in standard German (Duden); "rare" is an exaggeration but harmless. "dass omitted -> V2 accepted": correct.
Findings:
- [B] "Se la subordinata viene prima, la principale riparte con il verbo ... (il verbo è in prima posizione, il soggetto dopo)": wrong terminology/analysis. The subordinate clause occupies position 1 and the finite verb is in position 2 (V2 is preserved); de-nebensaetze1 says it correctly. Fix the sentence.
- [B] damit is taught only as "soggetti diversi" (table + c2) and "um...zu stesso soggetto, damit soggetti diversi" as if mandatory. damit with the same subject is perfectly grammatical (Ich lerne Deutsch, damit ich in Wien arbeiten kann), especially with modals; the true rule is only that um...zu REQUIRES the same subject. Rephrase as "damit è obbligatorio con soggetti diversi, possibile anche con lo stesso".
- [C] q7 expl "La b davanti a w si ammorbidisce in p": it is hardened (Auslautverhärtung, syllable-final b -> p), not softened; and it is the syllable ob-, not "davanti a w".
- [C] "obwohl: benché, nonostante": nonostante (preposition) = trotz; obwohl is only the conjunction. Add "trotz + Gen." elsewhere (it is in de-relativ).
- [C] Pronunciation notation in this B module uses "E:" for both closed e (GE:-ë) and ä (VE:-rënt, fer-SHTE:ST) and lowercase "e:r", "iç", while the A modules use É: / È: and "ER". The legend says E = short open. Readers following the legend will read "E:" ambiguously. (Header of tedesco.b.ts already admits conventions not in legend.)
- [C] "Es war so laut, dass ich nichts verstehen konnte": fine. "Er geht, ohne sich zu verabschieden": fine.
Quiz q1-q9 keys correct; no second correct option (q2 damit only; q1 um only). Practice p1-p6 correct.
